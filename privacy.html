GLN Rclone Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Purpose

GLN Rclone is a private application used solely by its owner to connect the rclone desktop program to the owner's Google Drive for personal file access, synchronization, and backup.

Google Drive data

The application accesses Google Drive data only when directed by its owner. Access may include viewing, creating, updating, downloading, and deleting files as required for file synchronization and backup.

Data collection and sharing

This application does not collect, sell, rent, share, or disclose Google user data to third parties. It has no public users, advertising, analytics, or external application server.

Credential storage

OAuth credentials and authorization tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer in the rclone configuration. They are not published on this website.

Data retention

This application does not maintain a separate database of Google user data. Files remain subject to the owner's Google Drive and local backup retention settings.
